# Choice — Company & Platform Overview

## What is Choice?

Choice (choiceqr.com) is an **all-in-one AI-native platform for restaurants, bars, cafés, and ghost kitchens** — one of the fastest-growing restaurant platforms built around AI.

Choice connects a restaurant with its customers across every touchpoint: it builds the restaurant's website, accepts delivery and takeaway orders at 0% commission, integrates with all major food delivery marketplaces (Wolt, Bolt, Foodora, Glovo, Uber Eats), connects to POS systems, manages table reservations, runs loyalty programs, and consolidates every customer interaction into a single owned database. Everything is managed from one admin panel — and everything can be controlled through AI.

Traditional POS systems automate the **back office**. Choice is the **front office** — the primary software restaurants use to communicate with guests, both inside and outside the restaurant. By covering every guest-facing channel in one place, Choice aggregates behavioral data across all touchpoints and helps restaurants grow customer retention.

**Tagline:** "The ultimate all-in-one solution for restaurants"

## What Problems Choice Solves

1. **High marketplace commissions** — Wolt, Glovo, Bolt etc. charge 25–35% per order. Choice lets restaurants take direct orders at 0% commission.
2. **Fragmented operations** — Managing orders from multiple sources (website, marketplaces, tables) across different devices. Choice unifies everything.
3. **Expensive tech stack** — Building a website, app, POS integration, loyalty program separately costs tens of thousands. Choice bundles it all from free (Basic) to €100/mo.
4. **No customer data** — Marketplace orders don't give restaurants their customers' data. Choice builds a owned customer database.
