## TL;DR
One tablet instead of many. Receive orders from all marketplaces and your own website in one place, manage the menu across all platforms from one admin, connect unlimited virtual brands, and optionally sync everything to your POS. Available on PRO (or as a standalone module).

## What It Is
Collection Point replaces all your marketplace tablets with one device. Orders from Wolt, Bolt, Foodora, Glovo, Uber Eats, Just Eat, LOKO, and your own delivery website all arrive in a single app. The menu is managed once and pushed to every connected platform. You can run multiple virtual brands from the same admin, toggle items on/off instantly, and see analytics for every channel side by side.

## Who It's For
- Running 2+ delivery platforms and tired of juggling multiple tablets
- Updating the same menu in multiple platform admin panels
- Running or planning to run virtual brands from one kitchen
- Using a POS and manually re-entering marketplace orders into it
- No clear picture of which platform actually performs best

## Key Value Points

**1. One tablet for everything**
All marketplace orders and own-website orders arrive in a single app on one device. Each order is labeled with the platform logo so staff always knows the source. No switching between apps or tablets.

**2. Manage menu across all marketplaces in one place**
Make a change once in Choice — it syncs to every connected platform automatically. Prices, names, dish availability, and modifiers can be set per platform independently. No logging into multiple admin panels.

**3. Instant on/off control**
Turn individual dishes, categories, or entire platforms on and off in seconds — directly from the Choice admin or the app. Run temporary menus, pause orders on one platform, or stop a sold-out item across all platforms at once.

**4. Unlimited virtual brands**
Connect any number of virtual brands to one Collection Point. Each brand has its own menu and marketplace presence but is managed from the same admin panel and received on the same device.

**5. POS integration**
Orders from all marketplaces pass directly into your POS — no manual entry, no errors. Supported POS systems: Dotykačka, Storyous, Poster, Syrve (IIKO), R-Keeper, Servio, Profit.

**6. Analytics for all channels in one place**
Compare order volume, average order value, and top-selling dishes across every marketplace and your own delivery — all in one dashboard. See which platform earns the most and make decisions based on real data.

## How It Works

1. Customer places an order on any connected marketplace or your own website
2. Order appears instantly in the Choice Business app — labeled with the platform logo
3. Staff confirms or auto-accept processes the order
4. If POS is connected — order goes directly into the POS, no manual entry needed
5. Menu changes made in Choice admin sync automatically to all connected platforms
6. Analytics dashboard shows performance across all platforms and channels in one view

## FAQ

**What is Collection Point?**
A feature that lets you receive and manage orders from all delivery marketplaces (Wolt, Bolt, Foodora, Glovo, Uber Eats, Just Eat, LOKO) and your own delivery website — all on one device, in one app.

**Can I connect my own delivery website?**
Yes — your own-channel orders appear alongside marketplace orders in the same interface.

**Can I run multiple virtual brands?**
Yes — connect unlimited virtual brands to one Collection Point and manage all of them from a single admin panel.

**How does POS integration work?**
Orders from all platforms are automatically sent to your POS when received — no manual re-entry, fewer errors.

**Can I edit the menu separately per marketplace?**
Yes — prices, names, and availability can be set independently per platform from one admin.

**Can I turn off a dish or platform quickly?**
Yes — toggle any item, category, or entire marketplace connection on or off instantly from the app or admin panel.

**Can I view analytics for all platforms together?**
Yes — order volume, average order value, revenue, and top dishes per platform all in one dashboard.

**How much does it cost?**
Included in Smart and Pro plans. Available as an add-on (€40/month + €80 setup) on Basic and Standard.

**What support is available after setup?**
Phone, email, messengers, and live chat.
