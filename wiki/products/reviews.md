# Product: Reviews Management

**Part of:** Marketing Tools / all plans
**URL:** https://choiceqr.com/products-en/marketing-for-restaurants/

## TL;DR
Automatically collect feedback after every order, visit, or reservation. Positive reviews are routed to Google; negative ones go privately to the manager. Main benefit: more Google reviews, fewer public complaints.

## What It Is
Smart review funnel: after each order or visit, the guest is automatically prompted for feedback. The system filters by sentiment and routes accordingly.

## Key Features

**Review Collection**
- Automatic feedback request after every order, visit, or reservation
- Anonymous or identified submissions
- No manual follow-up needed

**Smart Routing**
- Positive → prompted to share on Google / website
- Negative → goes to manager inbox privately for resolution

**Notifications**
- Real-time Telegram alert when a new review arrives
- Manager can respond directly from notification

**Management Dashboard**
- All reviews with order/table context
- Respond from admin panel
- Filter by rating, date, channel

**Integrations**
- Google Maps — positive reviews forwarded automatically; improves Google rating over time
- Review display on Choice website and mobile app

## How It Works
1. Guest completes order or visit
2. Auto prompt for feedback (QR / email)
3. Positive → "Share on Google" shown
4. Negative → goes to manager privately
5. Manager resolves; issue handled before going public

## Business Impact
| Metric | Improvement |
|--------|------------|
| Review volume increase | ~20% |
| Negative reviews caught before going public | ~60% |

## FAQ (from website)
**How does it work?**
Guests leave reviews after a visit or order via QR, online menu, or mobile app. All reviews appear in admin dashboard and Choice Business app — view and reply via Telegram or email.

**Can reviews be displayed on the website?**
Yes — publish in your app and encourage guests to share positive ones on Google Maps and TripAdvisor.

**Can reviews be submitted anonymously?**
Yes — guests can submit anonymously or with contact details.

**Can I reply to reviews from the dashboard?**
Yes — directly from admin panel.

**How to activate?**
Basic plan+. Go to "Features" → "Reviews."

**Setup time:** 1 day.

## Plan Availability
Available on **all plans** (Basic, Standard, Smart, Pro).
