## TL;DR
Built-in loyalty: cashback, points, gifts, promo codes — motivates repeat orders directly and builds independence from delivery platforms. Customers who earn rewards through your channel reorder directly, not through Wolt.

## What It Is
The restaurant configures rules once — then every order automatically earns the customer points or cashback, visible in their personal account. Works across delivery, takeaway, and dine-in. No external tool needed.

## Features

**Cashback**
- % of each order credited to customer wallet
- Works across delivery, takeaway, QR (dine-in)
- Example: "10% cashback on all direct orders this month"

**Points System**
- Earn points per order or per item
- Customers redeem points for discounts or free items
- Can be customized: percentage of order amount or fixed points per dish
- Reward rules can differ by channel (dine-in vs. delivery vs. takeaway)

**Gifts / Presents**
- Triggered automatically (e.g. free item when order > 500 CZK / €20)
- Or manually sent to specific customer segments

**Promo Codes**
- Single-use or multi-use, percentage or fixed discount
- Distribute via email, SMS, or printed on packaging
- Example: "SAVE10 — 10% off your first direct order"

**SMS & Email Campaigns**
- Retention messages to lapsed customers ("We miss you — here's 15% off")
- Seasonal promos sent directly to the restaurant's owned database

**Cross-location Loyalty**
- For chains: earn and redeem across all locations
- Unified loyalty for multi-brand groups (Pro plan)

Yes — percentage of order amount or fixed points per dish.

**Works for online orders?**
Yes — points accumulate and redeem via QR codes, online menu, or mobile app.

Yes — send promo offers to regular guests via email or push notifications.
