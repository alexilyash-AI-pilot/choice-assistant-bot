## TL;DR
Marketing tools (CRM, promo codes, loyalty/cashback, gifts, mailing, push notifications, SEO) help the restaurant build its own customer database and bring customers back directly — instead of losing them to platforms. **SMART / PRO plans.**

## What It Is
Tools for owning customer relationships instead of depending on delivery platforms. Collect data automatically from all channels, motivate repeat orders, run campaigns, and track results — all from one admin panel.

**Included:**
- **Customer database (CRM)** — contacts, order history, preferences
- **Promo codes and discounts**
- **Loyalty program** — cashback (e.g. 10%), points system
- **Gifts** on order (e.g. free item over 500 CZK / €20)
- **Bulk email mailing** to registered customers
- **SMS marketing** — retention and promotions (Smart/Pro)
- **Push notifications** — in-app and browser push for promotions and order updates
- **SEO tools** — built-in optimization, meta tags, Google integration
- **Analytics dashboard** — traffic, conversions, order trends, campaign effectiveness

Data is automatically collected from all channels — **QR, delivery, takeaway, and reservations** — into one database.

## Real Example
A sushi restaurant sends orders through Wolt paying 30% commission. They partner with **Studio TD** who makes branded packaging boxes. Each box has a **QR code** leading to their Choice e-shop with: *"Use code SAVE10 on your first order and get 10% cashback."*

Next time the customer scans the QR instead of opening Wolt → gets a discount → earns cashback → orders directly. The restaurant gets the contact (own CRM), order history, and ability to reach the customer via mailing. This is how a restaurant **gradually reclaims customers from Wolt into its own database**.
