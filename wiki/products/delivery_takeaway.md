## What It Is
Own branded ordering system for delivery and takeaway. No per-order commissions. Option to connect Wolt Drive couriers at a fixed price, own couriers, or other on-demand courier services.

## Who It's For
- Restaurants paying 25–35% commission on orders through third-party delivery platforms
- Venues with loyal customers who want a direct ordering channel
- Restaurants handling phone orders manually (errors, missed orders)
- Anyone who wants to own their customer data and relationships

**Branded Experience**
- Fully branded website and ordering flow in your colors and logo
- ~20% conversion rate on order completion

## FAQ

**How does it work?**
Customer orders on restaurant website (delivery or pickup) → restaurant receives it in Choice Business app → accepts and processes.

**How can customers pay?**
Card, Apple Pay/Google Pay, or cash on delivery.

**How is delivery organized?**
Own couriers, or connect Wolt Drive, Stuart, Glovo On-Demand, Uber Direct, Foodora Go, or Uklon (Ukraine).

**Can I manage menu and prices myself?**
Yes — all changes in admin panel, instantly visible to guests.

**Can I view sales reports?**
Yes — orders, average check, revenue, delivery/pickup/marketplace split, popular dishes.

**How long to launch?**
Usually 1–5 days after registration and menu upload.

**Is additional equipment required?**
No — works on smartphone, tablet, or computer.
