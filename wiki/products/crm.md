## TL;DR
Choice automatically builds the restaurant's own customer database from every order and interaction — no manual work. All customer data belongs to the restaurant, not to the platforms.

## What It Is
Most restaurants have no guest database at all. They serve hundreds of people every week but have no way to reach them again — no contacts, no history, no way to send a promotion or bring someone back.

Choice builds that database automatically. Every order through Choice (QR, delivery, takeaway, reservations) adds or updates the customer's profile — no manual work. Over time the restaurant gets a full, owned list of their real customers: who they are, what they order, when they last visited. That list can be used to send special offers, run promotions, and work on retention — turning one-time guests into regulars.

## Key Features

**Customer Database**
- Name, phone, email captured automatically on every order
- Full order history and spending patterns per customer
- Favourite dishes and preferences tracked
- Staff notes per customer (seating preferences, allergies, habits)
- Review history linked to customer profile
- Export to XLSX / CSV at any time — data is always yours

**Analytics & Insights**
- Menu popularity from real order data
- Channel comparison: dine-in vs. delivery vs. takeaway
- New vs. returning customer breakdown
- Marketing campaign effectiveness tracking

**Marketing from the Database**
- Email / bulk mailing to registered customers
- Push notification campaigns
- Promo codes and loyalty program integration
- Segmentation by: new guests, last order date, order frequency, spend level (Smart/Pro)
- Automated retention: "We miss you" message triggered after configurable inactivity period

## FAQ

**What data is stored?**
Contact details, order history, preferences, reviews, addresses, loyalty points, notes.

**Can I import/export the database?**
Yes — XLSX or CSV format. Data belongs to the restaurant permanently.

**Can I message customers from the CRM?**
Yes — email and push notifications with promotions, reminders, special offers.

**How is data collected?**
Automatically from every order across all channels: QR table orders, delivery, takeaway, and reservations. No manual entry needed.

**How to activate?**
Smart plan+. Go to "Clients" → "Customer Database."

**Setup time?**
No additional setup — activate and use immediately.
