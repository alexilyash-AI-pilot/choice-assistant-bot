# Product: QR Orders to Table

**URL:** https://choiceqr.com/products-en/qr-orders-to-table/

## What It Is
Guests scan a QR code at the table and order directly from their phone. The order goes straight to the bar or kitchen — staff accept it and bring the food.

QR Orders doesn't replace waiters — it helps them. One waiter can handle a large terrace or more tables at once without needing extra staff during peak hours. Instead of taking orders, staff focus on running food and taking care of guests. You need more runners, fewer order-takers.

## How It Works
1. Guest scans QR code at their table
2. Browses the menu — with photos and in their language
3. Places order → appears instantly at the bar or kitchen (POS / staff tablet)
4. Staff accepts and prepares
5. Food is brought to the table

## Key Features
- **Per-table QR codes** — each table has a unique code; order is tied to the table number
- **No app download** — works in any mobile browser
- **Menu in any language** — automatic translation into 99 languages; guests order in their own language
- **Photos for every dish** — guests see what they're ordering
- **POS integration** — orders go straight to kitchen display or POS; no manual entry
- **Accept/reject orders** — staff manage order flow directly in the app
- **Upsell engine** — dish recommendations and wishlist increase average check; guests browse at their own pace and order extras they might not have asked a busy waiter for
- **Tipping** — digital tip prompts with configurable percentages; guests tip more when it's one tap
- **Online payment** — guests can pay via QR at checkout or cash at the end
- **Customer data** — every order automatically builds the CRM database
