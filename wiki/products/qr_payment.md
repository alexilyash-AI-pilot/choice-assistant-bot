# Product: QR Payments at the Table

**URL:** https://choiceqr.com/products-en/qr-payments-at-the-table/

## TL;DR
Guests pay at the table by scanning a QR code — no waiting for the check, no cash, no terminal. Bill closes in the POS automatically. Available on all plans.

## What It Is
QR Payment doesn't change how the waiter works — staff take orders and add them to the POS as normal. The only difference is at the end: instead of waiting for the waiter to bring the terminal, guests scan the QR code at the table and pay themselves.

## How It Works
1. Waiter serves the table and adds all orders to the POS as usual
2. When guests are ready to leave, they scan the QR code at the table
3. They see their full itemized bill (pulled directly from the POS)
4. Each guest selects what they want to pay, adds a tip if they want
5. Pay with Apple Pay / Google Pay or card — in seconds
6. Bill closes automatically in the POS; receipt sent to guest's email
7. Staff gets an instant notification — table is marked as paid

## Key Features
- **No app, no registration** — works in any mobile browser, with any bank
- **Apple Pay & Google Pay** — one tap payment
- **Bill splitting** — large tables split individually; each person pays their own dishes
- **Per-person tipping** — each guest tips independently; guests are presented with tip options automatically
- **Real-time POS sync** — payment closes the bill in Poster, Syrve, Storyous, Dotykačka, R-Keeper, etc.
- **Email receipt** — sent to guest automatically after payment
- **Instant staff notification** — table marked as paid the moment payment goes through
- **Customer data capture** — builds CRM database automatically with each payment
- **Loyalty program** — points and cashback collected automatically on payment

## Benefits
