## TL;DR
Online reservation system with calendar, automatic confirmation, deposits against no-shows, and a single guest database. **Available from STANDARD** (or as standalone Reservation module). Guests book anytime — even after closing hours — without calling.

## What It Is
Online reservation system with an interactive calendar, automatic booking confirmation, capacity management, optional deposits, and integration with other Choice tools (web, QR, delivery) — all synced into one customer database.

## Who It's For
- Taking reservations by phone, Instagram, or paper book
- Experiencing no-shows or holding tables unnecessarily
- Wanting an overview of occupancy, possibly across multiple branches
- Currently paying for an expensive reservation-only system

## Key Features

**Booking Channels**
- Own website embedded widget
- Direct shareable link (Instagram, Facebook, etc.)
- QR code (printed at entrance or on tables)
- **Google Business Profile "Reserve a Table" button** — guests book directly from Google search
- Any channel without needing a separate website

**Management**
- Interactive calendar with table map / floor plan
- Day-by-day management with shift and time-slot control
- Automatic confirmation sent to guest via email or SMS
- Reminders automatically reduce no-shows
- Post-visit review request sent automatically
- **Deposits or card guarantee** to prevent no-shows

**Guest Database**
- Single database built automatically across all channels (QR, delivery, reservations)
- Works across multiple branches with central overview
- Staff notes per guest (preferences, allergies, special occasions)

**No Separate Website Needed**
- Booking link works independently — restaurants without a website can still accept online reservations

## FAQ

**How does the reservation system work?**
Guests select date, time, and number of people, enter contact details, and confirm. The restaurant receives a notification in the Choice Business app.

**Does it integrate with POS?**
No — the reservation system does **not** integrate with POS systems.

**Is additional equipment required?**
No — works on any device with internet (smartphone, tablet, computer).

**How long to set up?**
One business day after registration.

**Can I add a deposit requirement for large groups?**
Yes — you can configure deposits or card guarantees per booking type or party size to protect against no-shows.

**Can guests book directly from Google?**
Yes — Choice integrates with Google Business Profile to show a "Reserve a Table" button directly in Google search results.

**How to activate?**
Go to "Features" → "Table Reservation" in your admin panel.
