## TL;DR
Digital menu accessible by scanning a QR code — guests browse the full menu with photos, videos, and automatic translation in 99 languages. No app, no registration. Available from BASIC plan.

## What It Is
A digital version of the restaurant's menu, accessible via QR code at the table or a direct link. Guests see the full menu with photos, descriptions, allergens, and can browse in their own language.

QR Menu is the base layer — it can be extended with:
- **[QR Orders](qr_orders_to_table.md)** — guests order directly from their phone
- **[QR Payments](qr_payment.md)** — guests pay the bill by scanning the QR at the table

## Who It's For
- Any restaurant, bar, or café that wants a modern digital menu
- Tourist locations where language is a barrier
- Venues wanting to eliminate printed menus and manual updates
- Restaurants already thinking about moving to QR orders or payments later

## Key Features
- **Instant access** — scan → full menu with photos and videos; no app, no registration
- **99-language automatic translation** — guests see the menu in their device's language
- **Rich media** — photos, videos per dish; ingredient and allergen info
- **Real-time updates** — change prices, availability, specials instantly; no reprinting
- **Promotional tools** — Happy Hours, Business Lunch banners, seasonal offers
- **Wishlist** — guests save dishes and show their selection to the waiter
- **Interactive info** — business hours, Wi-Fi password, address, social links

## Extending QR Menu
Once QR Menu is set up, activating QR Orders or QR Payments requires no new hardware or menu setup — everything is already in place. Restaurants often start with QR Menu and add ordering or payments once staff and guests are comfortable.

## FAQ

**Do customers need to download an app?**
No — just scan the QR code. No registration required.

**How long does setup take?**
1–5 days after registration and menu upload.

**Does it support multiple languages?**
Yes — automatic translation into 99 languages.

**Can I update the menu myself?**
Yes — any change in the admin panel is live immediately, no technical knowledge needed.

**Can I add videos to dishes?**
Yes — each dish can have a photo or video.
