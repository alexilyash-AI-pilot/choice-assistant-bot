## Why Choice vs Each Competitor

**vs Qerko**
Qerko solves one thing — QR payments at the table. Choice does the same, at a lower transaction fee, plus you get your own website, online ordering, delivery integrations, loyalty program, and CRM. With Qerko, your customer data stays in their database — you only see a first name and last payment time. With Choice, you own your full customer database: names, contacts, complete order history — ready to use for marketing and retention. And Qerko shows your guests promotions from other restaurants inside their app. With Choice, your guests stay in your brand.

**vs DISH (Makro/Metro)**
DISH is the cheapest option on the market — but it shows. One website template, no POS integration, no Apple Pay, no Wolt Drive courier. Fine for a restaurant that wants the bare minimum. Choice gives you a modern platform that actually grows with your business.

**vs Objednáme.cz**
Objednáme focuses on delivery and takeaway orders, but has no QR table system, no reservations, and weak marketing tools. If you want to cover everything — dine-in, delivery, and direct customer relationships — Choice is the better fit.

**vs Speedlo**
Speedlo charges a percentage of every order through their portal on top of a monthly fee. That adds up fast. Choice has a flat subscription — no commissions, no surprises. And Speedlo doesn't offer QR table ordering or reservations.

**vs Adaptee**
Adaptee is solid for delivery (Wolt Drive included), but it doesn't cover dine-in at all — no QR orders, no QR payments, no reservations. If your restaurant earns from both delivery and guests sitting at tables, Adaptee only solves half your problem.

**vs Restia**
Restia handles delivery orders and Collection Point, but that's it. No website, no table QR, no reservations, no loyalty. It's a narrow tool, not a platform.

**vs BookioPro**
BookioPro is a good reservation system — no commission per booking, which is fair. But it's reservations only. No online ordering, no QR menu, no loyalty, no CRM. Choice includes a full reservation system as part of a much broader platform.

**vs Apetee**
Apetee is a reservation portal and restaurant catalogue. When a guest books through Apetee, they're on Apetee's platform — not yours. You don't get their data, you don't build your brand. With Choice, the guest interacts directly with your restaurant.

**vs Rej Dílky**
Rej Dílky is a PR and portal tool — good for visibility, but it doesn't take orders, doesn't run loyalty, and sends reminders to guests in its own name. Completely separate from your restaurant operations.

**vs Tableo**
Tableo is an international reservation tool with a free tier. It does one thing — reservations. Limited local support, nothing beyond booking management.

**vs Restaumatic**
Restaumatic charges a percentage of every order on top of a fixed fee. Support is only available Mon–Fri until 14:00. Reservations only work via email. Payment system messages appear in Polish even in Slovak restaurants. Choice offers daily support until 22:00, a flat subscription with no order commissions, and a system built for your local market.

### Why Choice vs Each Competitor (Poland)

**vs Restaumatic** — Poland market leader in online ordering
Restaumatic charges ~4% of revenue — the more you grow, the more you pay. Choice is a flat subscription with no order commissions. As your restaurant scales, your Choice fee stays exactly the same. Restaumatic also has no QR table payments, no real table reservations, and no chain ordering. Their Pyszne.pl marketplace integration is known to be unreliable. Choice gives you a modern platform built for growth — not a website with a basic ordering form.

**vs UpMenu** — broad features, no local team
UpMenu charges based on order count — as you grow past their plan limits, your bill grows. Choice has no order caps: pay once, use without limits. UpMenu has no chain ordering (cannot route orders across locations), no table reservations, and no local Poland support team. Their marketplace aggregation is handled by third-party contractors — not their core product.

**vs Restimo** — strong in marketplace aggregation, everything else in beta
Restimo's marketplace aggregation is solid — but their online ordering, CRM, and marketing tools are all in beta. Choice is a complete, live platform. Restimo also charges per order volume tier — as your business grows, you get bumped to a more expensive plan. Choice stays flat. If you're comparing on aggregation alone, ask what you'll use for ordering, loyalty, and marketing — and what that costs separately.

**vs GlobalTips** — QR tips only, no local support
GlobalTips does one thing: QR tips and payments. Choice includes QR ordering, QR payments, tips, delivery, reservations, and marketing — all in one flat subscription. GlobalTips has no local team in Poland. Choice has sales and CSM on the ground in Polish cities.

## Ukraine Market (UA)

> Main competitors in Ukraine: **Poster, Monobank, Eatery Club, GetOrder, Redstone, E-app, Dots**

### Why Choice vs Each Competitor (Ukraine)

**vs Poster**
Poster is primarily a POS system — it added ordering and QR menu as extras, each billed separately (Poster QR + Poster Site + individual integrations for Glovo, Bolt, Uklon). The costs stack up fast. Choice bundles everything in one flat subscription. Poster also has no loyalty program, no automated menu translation, and no marketing tools like email or push notifications. With Choice, you get a complete guest-facing platform — not a POS with add-ons bolted on.

**vs Monobank (QR ordering)**
Monobank offers QR menu and payment at ~1.3% commission — similar to our Basic plan. But that's all it does. No website, no delivery ordering, no loyalty, no CRM, no marketing, no reservations, no chain management, no automatic menu translation. It's a narrow payment tool. Choice covers the full restaurant-guest relationship.

**vs Eatery Club**
Eatery Club has a decent feature set — website, QR menu, loyalty, marketing. But no marketplace integrations (no Glovo, Bolt), no Uklon courier, no automatic menu translation (99 languages), and no dedicated account manager. Choice covers everything Eatery Club does, plus delivery marketplace aggregation and full courier integrations.

**vs GetOrder**
GetOrder charges by order volume — the more orders you get, the more you pay (up to 4,500 UAH/month per location, plus 4,000 UAH per virtual brand). As you grow, your bill grows. Choice is a flat subscription regardless of order count. GetOrder also has no loyalty system, no email/push marketing, and no menu translation. Choice is the better fit for restaurants that want to scale.

**vs Redstone**
Redstone is a custom development agency — they build bespoke restaurant websites. That means high upfront cost (~$3,000+), slow timelines, and ongoing fees for every change. No built-in loyalty, no marketplace integrations, no automated marketing. Choice is ready in 48 hours, includes everything, and costs a fraction of custom development.

**vs E-app**
E-app is a subscription platform with a separate charge for their mobile app ($100/month extra). No automatic menu translation, no Glovo/Bolt integration, no dedicated account manager. Choice includes all of this in the flat plan — the mobile app is included in Pro without extra monthly fees.

**vs Dots**
Dots has a broad feature set and competitive pricing ($49–$169/month). Their weakness: no Glovo/Bolt marketplace integration, no automatic menu translation (99 languages), no dedicated CSM, and no Choice Business app for order management. Choice is stronger on marketplace aggregation, chain management, and ongoing support.
