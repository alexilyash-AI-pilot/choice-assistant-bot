# Delivery & Couriers

Common issues with couriers, delivery problems, and Wolt Drive.

---

## Where is the Wolt Drive courier? Courier is delayed.

**Track the courier:**
- Open Choice Business app → WOLT section → click **"Track"** button to see live courier location

**Courier delayed or need to change delivery address/time:**
- Click the **"Support"** button in the Choice Business app → Choice support team will forward the information to Wolt Drive

**Do not contact Wolt Drive directly for order changes** — go through Choice support.

---

## Courier picked up order but didn't deliver it

1. **Contact Choice support immediately** with order details (order ID, time, address)
2. Choice will contact the delivery service to get the courier's status and determine if delivery is still possible
3. If delivery is no longer possible: you can **cancel the order** or Choice can process a **refund for the customer**
4. Then contact the delivery service via their **official email** with order details to request compensation if needed

---

## Wolt Drive refund process

If an order was not delivered and a refund is needed:
- Contact Choice support with the order details
- Choice coordinates the refund process with Wolt Drive
- Refund is processed back to the customer's original payment method

---

## How to connect third-party couriers

If you don't have own couriers, you can connect courier services via API at a **fixed price per delivery** (not commission-based).

Available services depend on country:
- **Wolt Drive** — CZ, SK, PL, HU, EE, LT, LV, RO, PT
- **Stuart** — selected EU markets
- **Glovo On-Demand** — selected EU markets
- **Uber Direct** — selected EU markets
- **Foodora Go** — selected EU markets
- **Uklon** — Ukraine

Configure in admin panel → Features → Delivery → Courier settings.
