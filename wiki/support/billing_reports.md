# Billing & Reports

Payout reports, invoices, and financial documents.

---

## Where to find payout reports

Admin panel → **Clients** → **Payouts**

- Reports are available in **PDF** or **Excel** format
- Transaction reports for the previous month are issued monthly by the **10th day of the new month**

**Important:** Payout reports include only orders and table reservations **paid online**. Cash orders are not included.

---

## Where to find invoices and tax documents

If your accountant or tax service requires a general invoice (not the payout report), contact your **personal manager** or the **technical support team** to request it.

General invoices are not self-service — they are issued on request.

---

## Cancelled order — will the customer get a refund?

Yes. If an order is cancelled after online payment, the payment is refunded to the customer's original payment method. Refund timing depends on the payment provider (Adyen for EU, LiqPay/Monobank for Ukraine) and the customer's bank — typically 3–10 business days.

For Wolt Drive courier issues where delivery didn't happen — see [Delivery & Couriers](delivery_couriers.md).
