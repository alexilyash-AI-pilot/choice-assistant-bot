# Servio POS Integration

## Overview

Servio integrates via OpenAPI. The entire setup is handled on the Servio side.

## How to Connect

1. Give Servio access to the customer's backoffice
2. Contact the Servio team via Telegram to push the integration through
3. Wait for Servio to complete the setup — no configuration needed on the Choice side

**Note:** The setup takes place entirely on the Servio side. Choice support's role is to provide backoffice access and follow up via Telegram chat.
